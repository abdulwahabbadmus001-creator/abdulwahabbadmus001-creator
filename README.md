**Research · Software · AI Governance · Public Policy**

I work across software development, historical and policy research, AI governance, and public-interest technology.

My independent projects often begin with a problem I want to understand. I use software to investigate practical questions around small-business operations, connectivity, language access, education, cultural preservation and knowledge infrastructure.

Alongside development, I research how African states can strengthen their technological and institutional capacity to participate more effectively in global AI governance.
