## About Me

My name is Wahab Opeyemi Badmus. I am a Nigerian researcher and writer working on AI governance and public policy in Africa. I serve as Director of Research and Development at The Visionnaires Nigeria and I am a member of Kectil Organisation International.

I write and publish research on Africa's AI governance frameworks, algorithmic bias, and global AI policy through Africa & Global Power, my independent research blog and newsletter. I also build web and mobile applications that put my research into practice, from education platforms to productivity tools.

I am pursuing postgraduate scholarships in AI, Governance, and Public Policy across the UK, Canada, and Europe.
